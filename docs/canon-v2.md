# Canon v2 — greenfield rewrite spec

Status: **superseded by rewrite-in-place (2026-07-29).** Ten writing nodes became eight vessels, each opened from dated contact in this repo. The six-essay greenfield (new ids, new titles) was not executed; the salvage operators and the contact constraint were. v1 copies live under `docs/drafts/v1-archive/`.

Binding constraint: **every essay earns external contact.** No essay may open by summarizing another essay. Each must touch something outside the corpus — a dated failure, a shipped artifact, a number, a scene, a source that changes the map rather than decorating it.

---

## Why greenfield (the finding that justifies it)

Ten essays. **Three ideas.**

- `bounded-me` → `geometry-retrieval` → `weak-geometry` → `allowed-ignorance` → `the-world-answers` is **one argument split five ways**, each installment opening with a ledger of the previous ones.
- `me-plus-ai` + `co-owning-the-loop` is **one control spec**, split badly — the 443-word piece has one distinct idea (tooling as institutional memory) buried in it.
- `tools-need-edges` is the only structurally independent essay.
- `ilya` (142w) and `increasing-returns` (264w) are **marginalia wearing essay chrome** — a quote plus a gloss.

A line-edit cannot fix a corpus whose defect is *how many essays there are*. That is the greenfield case.

Secondary defects confirmed on read:

1. **Vessel monoculture.** Four essays open `## Preamble`; three of those open with the identical backlink ledger. The voice skill's own rule — *if two recent essays used the same vessel, choose a different one* — is violated ten times out of ten.
2. **Line-break saturation.** One sentence per line at 100% density. The device was for isolating structural lines. At full saturation nothing can be isolated, because there is no ground left to place a figure against.
3. **Inbreeding.** `the-world-answers` argues that a map must be *touched by the world* and cites almost nothing but the other essays. The corpus theorizes feedback inside a room with no feedback.
4. **The literature tour.** `the-world-answers` opens sections from Peirce, Wiener, Ashby, Conant, Friston, Noë, Alexander, Schön, Levin, and Deming — **ten traditions in one essay.** The voice skill bans this pattern by name.

## The canon

Six essays. Argument spine: **container → cut → own → weaken → contact → couple**, plus one design essay that stands outside the chain.

Vessel sequence: **Glossary → Plate → Protocol → Dialogue → Case study → Control spec.** No repeats anywhere, not merely non-adjacent.

---

### 1. `the-container` — *hard limits and the ones I can move*

- **Vessel:** Glossary. Operators defined as usable tools, each with its wound.
- **Replaces:** `bounded-me`.
- **Opening pressure:** not "I keep getting tempted by clean definitions" (kept, but demoted). The opening is a **week that diverged**, named and dated — the good week and the trash week that differed by one meeting.
- **Thesis:** Boundedness is not a mood. It is a container with a fixed wall and a trainable interior. Training optimizes DLP. It never buys more HSL. When active edges exceed what the container holds, the move is **cut, not expand**.
- **External contact required:** Bennett 2026 (D ≤ kvθ) — already held, keep. **Plus one real logged comparison:** two weeks, what differed, what it cost.
- **Why glossary:** the HSL/DLP distinction is a set of *terms*, and terms want definitions with consequences attached. Currently they are an appendix bullet list — the glossary vessel is what they were always asking for.
- **Leaves behind:** the container audit. *List active edges. Count them against the window. Cut, do not expand.*

### 2. `the-cut` — *what I removed, and whether it survived*

- **Vessel:** Plate essay. Visual-first; the figures carry the argument.
- **Replaces:** `allowed-ignorance`.
- **Opening pressure:** a **crack**. One dated case where a difference that got collapsed came back as a fracture along a line previously ruled cosmetic.
- **Thesis:** A cut is not omission. It is *equivalence-making* — the licensing of a collapse. The void is functional and it is also where the next failure is hiding.
- **External contact required:** the dated fracture. Not a hypothetical, not a metaphor about maps. **A real thing that broke.**
- **Why plate:** `plate-cut.svg`, `plate-rotation.svg`, `plate-crack.svg` already exist and already carry the three movements. The current essay treats them as illustration. Invert it — let the plates carry, and cut the prose that duplicates them.
- **Leaves behind:** the rotation test. *If it disappears when turned, I only owned a face.*

### 3. `source-closed` — *the reconstruction protocol*

- **Vessel:** Protocol. Mostly tests, procedure, operating discipline.
- **Replaces:** `geometry-retrieval`.
- **Opening pressure:** a **logged failure of the test itself** — a case where the rebuild was attempted with the source closed and did not come back.
- **Thesis:** Recognition is cheap now. Reconstruction still costs something. If I can rebuild with the source closed, I have geometry; otherwise I have retrieval wearing its clothes.
- **External contact required:** Rozenblit & Keil 1998 (keep — it earns its place, it names the exact failure). **Plus the logged failure.** The current essay asserts the illusion of explanatory depth; it never shows Toni falling into it.
- **Why protocol:** the piece is already 60% procedure — the six tests, the four-step reconstruction loop, the edge-typing exercise. Stop apologizing for that with essay chrome and let it be a card.
- **Leaves behind:** the six tests, as a card that fits on one screen.

### 4. `weak-enough` — *the argument against my own weakness*

- **Vessel:** Dialogue. Claim and counter-claim; self-cross-exam.
- **Replaces:** `weak-geometry`.
- **Opening pressure:** the counter-position. **Weak geometry has a failure mode and the current essay never names it:** a map held weak forever is a map that never commits, and never committing is its own way of avoiding contact.
- **Thesis:** *(unresolved by design — this is the one essay that should end with pressure still live.)* Weakness is a discipline, not a temperament. The question the dialogue is trying to settle: what would legitimately harden an edge?
- **External contact required:** **a decision where holding the map weak cost something.** This is the hardest slot to fill and the most valuable. Without it the essay is the current one with quotation marks added.
- **Why dialogue:** `weak-geometry` is the thinnest-argued piece in the corpus (1,477 words that mostly restate three other essays). It has no opposition in it. Giving it an opponent is the only thing that will make it an essay.
- **Leaves behind:** the relax test — *if weakening one sentence destroys the structure, I do not have geometry* — but now with its cost priced.

### 5. `the-world-gets-a-turn` — *one probe, one return*

- **Vessel:** Case study. Project/interface/event first, principle second.
- **Replaces:** `the-world-answers`.
- **Opening pressure:** **the artifact.** One shipped thing. What was predicted, what was probed, what came back without permission, what the map had to stop pretending.
- **Thesis:** A map cannot certify itself from the inside. The contact chain (invariant → symmetry → compression → prediction → action → feedback → boundary) is the *output* of the case, arriving in the last third — not the frame that opens it.
- **External contact required:** **which artifact.** Candidates already in the pool: `synapse`, `the-loom`, `media-atlas`, `codex-fieldwork`, `geometry` itself. Needs a real prediction, a real probe, and a real return.
- **Citation budget: two.** Down from ten. The current essay is the corpus's worst voice violation — it opens six sections from a tradition. Keep the two whose absence would change the argument; the rest were authority, not instrumentation.
- **Why case study:** an essay whose entire claim is *ideas need surfaces* cannot be argued from citations. The vessel has to enact the thesis.
- **Leaves behind:** the contact protocol, as seven questions.

### 6. `load-order` — *which instrument, when*

- **Vessel:** Control spec. Rules, gates, levels, failure modes, hard stops.
- **Replaces:** `me-plus-ai` + absorbs `co-owning-the-loop`.
- **Opening pressure:** **the stabilizer became tax.** Not "I am not using AI, I am regulating a coupled feedback system" — that is a thesis announcement. The pressure is the moment the gates started costing more than the drift they prevented.
- **Thesis:** *Stacking is how verification becomes heavier than the loop it was meant to protect.* The gates are layered instruments with a load order, not a stack run on every thought.
- **External contact required:** **a dated drift episode** — coupling that tightened, what it cost, how it was caught. Plus Bennett's latency ceiling (BCI 10 ms vs the 20–50 ms window) which is already the strongest external fact in the corpus.
- **Structural note:** the current essay is 1,995 words across eleven sections and buries its best idea at §9. **Invert.** Load order is the thesis; L0–L3, R3+2+1, the drift parameters, and the hard stops become the instruments the load order sequences. Absorb co-ownership's *hooks as institutional memory* as the reason a load order can be encoded rather than remembered.
- **Leaves behind:** the load-order card — which gate on a decision, on a topology, on an ownership claim, on an external bet, on drift.

## Migration mechanics

Greenfield touches more than `content/writing/`. Verified coupling:

**Retiring 10 ids, introducing 6.** Two ids could be kept (`bounded-me`, `weak-geometry`) if you want URL continuity, but the new titles are better and the pieces are genuinely different objects.

1. **`src/pool/field.ts`** — 10 hand-placed `positions` entries at lines 8–17 must be replaced with 6. Two `nodeIds` cluster arrays (lines ~56–74) reference the retiring ids, including `co-owning-the-loop` in a second cluster at line 74. `AGENTS.md` states id must match a `positions` key — **`pool:build` will fail otherwise.**
2. **`constellation/sources/*.json`** — all 10 regenerate. `constellation:build` **fails** if hydration cannot produce a lens node plus section-anchored inquiries, so each new essay needs its `##` spine settled before the graph is authored.
3. **Inbound cross-cluster links** — three work nodes link to `geometry-retrieval`: `content/work/synapse.md:12`, `content/work/geometry.md:14`, `content/work/the-loom.md:14`. These must retarget to `source-closed`.
4. **Build gate** — `pool:build` fails if a constellation essay lacks `##`/`###` sections. Every new essay needs a real section spine, not just prose.
5. **Redirects** — `dist/_redirects` is currently a bare SPA catch-all (`/* /index.html 200`). If any `/read/:id` slug has been published or linked, add explicit redirects for the ten retiring slugs before shipping.
6. **`struct` frontmatter** — optional, but it powers the field-graph spine. Author it fresh; do not port.

**Order of operations:**

```
fill contact slots → draft 6 essays → settle ## spines
→ update field.ts positions + nodeIds
→ retarget the 3 inbound work links
→ pnpm pool:build && pnpm constellation:build
→ author constellation sources → rebuild
→ add redirects for the 10 retired slugs
→ pnpm test && pnpm build
```

## Line-level rules for the drafts

Carried from `.agents/skills/toni-ltd-voice/SKILL.md`, plus two corrections this audit adds:

- **Break the line-break saturation.** One sentence per line is a *figure*, not a *ground*. Target: no more than a third of any essay in isolated lines. Where the current corpus is at 100%, the isolated line has stopped meaning anything.
- **No essay opens by summarizing another essay.** The backlink ledger is retired as an opening move. Backlinks appear mid-essay, where they are load-bearing, or not at all.
- Name the operator; do not bury it in an explanatory clause.
- Every essay leaves a diagnostic, protocol, or hard stop.
- Keep claims weaker than the prose wants until contact earns strength.
- End with a live pressure when that is truer than closure — `weak-enough` is the one essay explicitly licensed to do this.
