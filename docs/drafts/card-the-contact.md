# Cards — the contact

Instruments belonging to *the contact*. Not essay prose — the objects it leaves
behind, and the ones absorbed from `me + AI` and `co-owning the loop` when those
retired.

---

## Load order — which instrument, when

The gates are not a stack to run on every thought. They are layered instruments with
triggers. Running all of them on everything is how a private stabiliser becomes a
second job, and the loop starts routing around it.

| trigger | instrument |
|---------|------------|
| always present, no ceremony | weak grades on edges I lean on; cuts I can still name |
| a decision | **R3+2+1** from memory |
| structure not yet a thesis | **the topology gate** |
| an ownership claim | **reconstruction** — close the source, rebuild, run the six tests |
| a bet on the world | **the probe** — one claim, one return, integration named first |
| drift detected | **recovery** — close, rewrite, re-enter at L0 or L1 |

Event-driven, never calendar-driven. A timer is how a gate becomes chrome.

If I cannot name which gate I am in, I am probably stacking them.

**A gate I skip is worse than a gate I never built, because I still get to feel
protected by it.** More verification is not monotonically safer.

## R3+2+1

Not allowed to increase coupling or finalise a piece unless this passes **from memory**:

1. **Thesis** — what am I claiming?
2. **Reason** — why do I believe it?
3. **Next action** — what decision does this change?

then

- **+2 assumptions** — what must be true for this to hold?
- **+1 uncertainty** — what am I least sure about?

Fail it and I decouple: drop to L1 or L0 and rebuild the shape myself. *I do not
prompt my way out of confusion. I rewrite my way out* — but only when something has
actually gone wrong, and not as a tax on every sentence that goes right.

### The topology gate

Some thinking is not ready to be a thesis. It is relational — nodes, edges, tensions,
an open connection that has not crystallised. For that work:

1. **Draw it** — can I sketch the structure from memory?
2. **Name the open edge** — what connection am I still testing?
3. **Falsify one link** — which relationship would break the map if wrong?

If I can, the understanding is mine before it is propositional. If I cannot, **I am
holding a shape I was handed.**

## The coupling gradient

**L0 Tool** — execution only, no influence on belief.
**L1 Scout** — the model expands the search space; I keep the conclusion.
**L2 Co-author** — the model writes; I constrain, audit, cut. Output is a draft, not an authority.
**L3 Integrated** — inside the decision loop. High bandwidth, high risk, sometimes the highest signal. Only with earned entry.

Landmarks, not rooms. The question is never *what level am I at* but **which direction
am I moving, and do I still know why.**

**Drift tells:** I stop rephrasing the output and start building on it · the model's
framing starts shaping the structure of my question · I feel pulled to prompt rather
than think · **I cannot restate my position without referencing the output.**

**Let it run when:** mapping a territory before the thesis exists · stress-testing a
framework · connecting domains I hold separately.

**Pull back when:** I defend the model's framing instead of testing it · I prompt
faster than I think · the conversation feels impressive and leaves no internal
geometry · I cannot name the next action without asking again.

Over-damping is a failure mode too — a system so regulated it cannot resonate.

---

## The four drift parameters

- **exchange** — bandwidth and persistence between me and the model
- **feedback control** — how well I can monitor, interpret, constrain, reject
- **latency** — how fast outputs alter my internal state
- **closure pressure** — how fast candidate structure hardens into belief

High exchange with weak feedback control is the danger zone. Low latency makes it
faster. Closure pressure makes it stick.

Models are **closure engines**: they make the first coherent map feel more finished
than it is. So — *increase exchange only when feedback control is increasing too.*

Drift is not a moral failure. It is a regime.

## Contact for abstract work

When the material is thin and there is nothing obvious to touch, four proxies, named
so I cannot skip them:

1. **Translation** — does the idea survive code, diagram, notation, another language?
2. **Social misfit** — does an independent observer resist or misread the compressed form?
3. **Temporal commitment** — is there a dated, observable prediction attached?
4. **Constraint stress** — does it survive no-AI, low time, or a foreign domain?

One probe is enough when the bet is small. A single edge takes temporary predictive
closure long enough to risk being wrong — then I downgrade or revise.

*Not a second framework. A practice card.*

## What a collaborator is

Not authority.
Not identity.
Not taste.

**Load-bearing participation.**

> A good AI collaborator does not replace judgement. It preserves the conditions under
> which judgement can keep showing up.

The partnership test, run on a working session: **did this increase exchange without
weakening feedback control?**

The division: the human keeps taste, values, and final judgement. The system carries
memory, checks, and repeatability. It works when both sides make the other more
capable without pretending to be the same thing.

A hook is a tiny piece of institutional memory. Enough of them and the codebase starts
pushing back when the loop gets sloppy — `prompt → edit → guard → check → ship → learn`.
That is when tooling stops being decoration and becomes a nervous system.

Tooling is culture because it turns preference into default behaviour. Direction over
speed. Repeatability over cleverness. **Trust is not a vibe — it is accumulated
evidence that the system catches me when attention drops.**

## The probe

*Run on a bet about the world — never on an ownership claim, which is what the
interior gates are for.*

1. **Claim** — what I think is true.
2. **Expectation** — what should change if it is.
3. **Probe** — the smallest action that lets the world answer.
4. **Return** — what came back *without my permission*.
5. **Integration** — where that answer lands, named in advance, before the probe runs.

Steps one to four are the part everyone runs. Step five is the one that makes the
other four worth running, and it is the only step the *Vasa* failed.

**A test tells you what is true. A channel is what carries the answer somewhere with
the authority to act on it.** Having the first and not the second is worth nothing,
and feels exactly like diligence.

## Hard stops

Not gates. They never fire conditionally.

- **No identity outsourcing.** The model does not answer who I am or what I should believe.
- **No reality arbitration.** It can summarise inputs. It cannot decide what happened.
- **Provenance is mandatory.** Source: human · assisted · co-authored. What was borrowed. What I rebuilt with the source closed.
- **No sensitive data handling.**

The principle underneath: **can verification help?** If yes, the output enters the
draft pipeline. If no, the output is *structurally unsafe* — and that is what makes a
stop a stop rather than a preference.

I do not need a verification UI. I need the seam to stay visible.
