Macroscopic is the largest version of the pattern: a product system for holding fragmented life and work context.

Its center is a cognitive kernel: events, active context, durable memory, missions, artifacts, connected sources, proposals, and approvals stay separate enough that the product can remain quiet without becoming opaque.

> [thesis|product bet]
> The useful assistant is not louder. It remembers what matters, surfaces only what earns attention, and asks before crossing an agency boundary.
